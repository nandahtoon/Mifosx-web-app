- Renewal review.

## Accounting Awareness

Financial workflow changes must be reviewed carefully because loan, savings, repayment, waiver, write-off, journal entry, and approval flows may affect accounting results.

## RiskOps Lite Domain

RiskOps Lite should explain operational risk signals. It should not perform credit decisions or financial actions.

## Rule

When business meaning is unclear, stop and ask for clarification before coding.
